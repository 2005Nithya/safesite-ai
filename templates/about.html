About SafeSite AI

AI-powered PPE compliance monitoring for construction sites.

Mission

Keeping every worker safe on every shift.

SafeSite AI uses a computer vision model trained on construction site imagery to detect workers and assess whether they are wearing the required protective gear — hard hats, vests, and other PPE. Upload a photo, a video, or stream from a live camera, and SafeSite AI flags non-compliant workers instantly.

How it works

  • Upload or stream images, video files, or live camera feeds.
  • AI detection identifies workers and PPE violations per frame.
  • Instant summary shows workers, safe workers, and violations.
  • Reports & analytics track compliance over time and export inspection PDFs.
🛡️

Image detection

Run PPE compliance checks on single photos from any site or device, with a downloadable annotated result.

🎥

Video detection

Analyse uploaded recordings frame by frame and get a full-site summary of detected workers and violations.

📡

Live monitoring

Connect a webcam or demo feed for real-time on-site monitoring with continuous PPE status updates.

Technologies

SafeSite AI is built on a Flask web server, a custom YOLOv8 computer vision model, OpenCV for video processing, and ReportLab for generating PDF inspection reports.
